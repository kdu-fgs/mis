Privacy Policy

Management Information System (MIS), Faculty of Graduate Studies, General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who we are

This web page belongs to the Faculty of Graduate Studies (FGS) of the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU). It is the sign-in page of the Faculty's Management Information System (MIS). You can reach us at graduatestudieskdu@gmail.com.

What this page is for

The page lets applicants, students, lecturers, examiners and Faculty staff who are registered in the MIS sign in with their Google account. They can then see the information that belongs to them, and authorised staff can approve requests and download reports. The page does not let anyone see information that their role does not allow.

What we receive from Google

When you sign in, Google tells us your name, your e-mail address and that the address belongs to you. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

What we do with it

Your e-mail address is sent to the Faculty's MIS, which checks whether it is registered. If it is, the MIS shows the pages and information that your role allows. If it is not, you are told that you have no access. We use your information only for the academic and administrative work of the Faculty.

Where information is kept

Student, staff and Faculty records are kept in Google Sheets and Google Drive files managed by the Faculty. The web page itself is hosted on GitHub Pages and holds no personal records.

What stays in your browser

While you are signed in, your browser keeps a sign-in token for the current tab. It is removed when you sign out or close the tab. This page does not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.

Records of actions

The MIS keeps an audit record of changes made through it (who made the change, what changed and when). This helps the Faculty keep its records correct and secure.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone outside the Faculty and the University, except where the law requires it. Google provides the sign-in, and GitHub hosts the files of this page.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information held about you, or to correct it, by writing to graduatestudieskdu@gmail.com. You can stop using the page at any time by signing out. You can also remove this page's access to your Google sign-in in your Google Account settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
